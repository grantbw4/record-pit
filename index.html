    About the data

    Amazon’s history supplies listening durations and catalog IDs. Some IDs lack a confirmed match to an album or artist name. Those events count toward total listening time, and coverage measures how much time could be assigned before limiting each view to its top 100.

    The song rankings include tracks with a resolved artist and album. Listening time includes partial plays and is capped at the listed track length when available. Album totals add up time spent on their tracks.
